How HemaScan works

Anaemia makes the inner eyelid (the palpebral conjunctiva) look pale, because there is less haemoglobin in the blood vessels there. Doctors check this by eye. HemaScan measures it from a photo.

1. Quality checkBrightness, blur, glare and how much eyelid is visible. Bad photos are rejected with a fix-it tip.
2. Tissue maskKeeps only pink conjunctiva pixels and removes reflections, eyelashes and skin.
3. Pallor featuresErythema index log₁₀(R/G) and CIELAB a* redness, both linked to Hb in published studies.
4. Hb estimateCalibrated regression, optionally combined with a fine-tuned MobileNetV3 CNN.
5. Explain + referWHO cut-offs by age, sex and pregnancy; plain-language advice by voice; nearest health centre.

Limitations

  • This is a screening tool. Only a blood test can diagnose anaemia.
  • Phone cameras and lighting change colours. Always use the flash and similar light.
  • Accuracy depends on calibration with real photos and lab Hb values (see train/).
